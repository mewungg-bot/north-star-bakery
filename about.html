Our story

Made by neighbors, for neighbors

North Star Bakery began with one starter, one borrowed oven, and a belief that good bread gives people a reason to pause together. Today, our small team still bakes in the same spirit: early mornings, careful fermentation, and a counter where familiar faces are always welcome.

A thoughtful approach to ingredients

We choose flour from regional mills when we can, buy seasonal fruit from nearby growers, and work with suppliers who care about the people and land behind each ingredient. Our menu follows the seasons instead of fighting them.

Because our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts, and other common allergens, please share allergy questions before placing a pre-order.

The people behind the counter

  • Mara, head baker: keeps our starter lively and believes every loaf deserves a dramatic crackle.
  • Jon, pastry lead: turns seasonal fruit into the danish that sells out first.
  • Our neighborhood crew: welcomes commuters, families, and event planners with the same care we put into every bake.
